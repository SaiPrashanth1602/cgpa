VIT GPA & CGPA Calculator

GradeOS helps VIT students calculate GPA, CGPA, and required target GPA instantly. The calculator supports semester GPA tracking, cumulative CGPA calculation, and future GPA prediction for placements and academic planning.

How GPA is Calculated

GPA is calculated using credit-weighted grade points for each subject. Higher grades in high-credit courses improve GPA significantly.

How CGPA Works

CGPA combines GPA scores from all semesters into one cumulative score. It reflects overall academic performance across your degree.

Why CGPA Matters

Many internships and placement opportunities use CGPA cutoffs during shortlisting and eligibility checks.
